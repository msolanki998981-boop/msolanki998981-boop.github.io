Frequently Asked Questions


Where is my data stored?

Your resume data is stored entirely on your Android device. We do not have servers that collect or store your resume information.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! The core resume builder and PDF exporter work completely offline. You only need an internet connection for advanced AI enhancements and unlocking premium templates via ads.

How do I unlock premium templates?

You can unlock premium templates by watching a short advertisement when prompted inside the app.
