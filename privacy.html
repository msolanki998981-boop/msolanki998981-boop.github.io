Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at AI Resume Maker. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Local-First and Offline Functionality

AI Resume Maker is built with a "Local-First" philosophy. All the data you enter to build your resume (such as your name, email, experience, education, and skills) is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or store your resume content on our servers.

2. Information Automatically Collected

We do not collect personal data from you directly. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings.

3. Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

  • Internet: Used to display advertisements and optional AI enhancements.
  • Storage: Used to save the PDF resumes generated by the app to your device storage.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the app does not collect personal data to our servers, we cannot hold such data. If we discover that any personal data was inadvertently sent to us by a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@airesume.app.
